# Catalog scope and reference notes

The catalog contains illustrative second-hand listings for UI development. It is
not a measured trading-volume ranking or a live Turkish market price feed. The
original 15 records retain their supplied prices; added records use synthetic TL
price ranges with minimum <= fair <= maximum. RAM prices represent the complete
kit shown in the product name. Laptop and phone prices refer to the listed
configuration or configuration family, not every variant sold under that name.

Added issue descriptions describe possible used-device inspection risks and
compatibility constraints. They do not establish defect prevalence for a model.
Repair notes request a diagnosis or quote instead of inventing repair prices.
Release years identify the model or family introduction, not the manufacture
date of an individual unit; memory revisions can differ. Alternative links are
purchase comparisons, not promises of socket, memory, or platform compatibility.
